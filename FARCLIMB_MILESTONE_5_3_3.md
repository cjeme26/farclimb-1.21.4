# FarClimb Milestone 5.3.3

This corrective build adjusts only the mantle landing position.

## Change

The mantle target now places the player centre 0.31 blocks inside the ledge face. With the normal player half-width of 0.30 blocks, this leaves only a very small safety margin on top of the block. The player should therefore finish visibly at the outer lip instead of being pulled toward the middle.

The 24-tick lift and 6-tick inward movement are unchanged.

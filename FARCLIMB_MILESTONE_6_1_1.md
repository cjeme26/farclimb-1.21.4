# FarClimb Milestone 6.1.1

Milestone 6.1.1 corrects the axe controls so the mouse buttons match the hands visible on screen.

## Control mapping

- Left click toggles the axe visible on the left side.
- Right click toggles the axe visible on the right side.
- With Minecraft's default right-handed setting, left click controls the offhand axe and right click controls the main-hand axe.
- With Minecraft's left-handed Main Hand setting, the mapping automatically reverses so the visible sides still match.
- Hand swing animations, one-axe pendulum behavior, two-axe climbing, mantling, and camera motion are unchanged.

## Suggested testing

1. With the default right-handed setting, left click the wall and confirm the left-side/offhand axe responds.
2. Right click and confirm the right-side/main-hand axe responds.
3. Release each axe using the same button that planted it.
4. Optionally change Options > Skin Customization > Main Hand to Left and verify the physical left/right mapping still matches the screen.
5. Confirm normal climbing, one-axe hanging, and mantling remain unchanged.
